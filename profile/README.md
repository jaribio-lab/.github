# Jaribio Lab

*Jaribio* is Swahili for **experiment**.

We run small, open experiments on AI and the brain, and share everything: code, data, configs, costs and results, including the ones that fail.

## What we do

- **Experiments.** We fine-tune and train models, then measure what actually happens. What does a model learn when you teach it wrong answers? Can GRPO teach a tiny model to reason? What really fits on a small edge device?
- **Explained.** Animated explainers of how things work, from a single neuron to backpropagation, PPO and GRPO.
- **Brain and silicon.** Where artificial neural networks match real neurons, and where the analogy breaks.

## Principles

- **Open by default.** Every experiment is reproducible from its folder.
- **Real numbers.** We report what it cost and what it ran on.
- **Honest results.** Negative results get published too.

## Repositories

- [experiments](https://github.com/jaribio-lab/experiments): one folder per experiment, ready to rerun
